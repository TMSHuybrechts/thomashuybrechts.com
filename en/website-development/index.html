Why a new website?

Most customers look you up on their phone first. If your site loads slowly, is hard to read on a small screen or they cannot find your phone number, they call the next one on the list. A good website solves exactly that: it loads fast, explains what you do in a few seconds and makes it easy to get in touch.

I build your site with clean, custom code instead of a heavy WordPress theme with dozens of plugins. That makes it fast, secure and cheap to maintain. You pay no subscription for a website builder and the site remains entirely yours.

Findable in Google, locally too

A beautiful site that nobody lands on earns nothing. That is why the basics of search engine optimisation (SEO) are included as standard: clear titles and descriptions per page, a logical structure, structured data for Google, a sitemap and a Google Search Console connection.

If you mainly work in your region, I focus the site on local searches, such as "plumber Geel" or "hairdresser Mol". Together with a Google Business Profile that is the quickest route to more calls from your own area.

Ready to grow with you

Your website is often the first piece of your digital business. If you later want questions answered automatically or enquiries to land straight in your calendar or CRM, I connect an AI chatbot or an n8n automation. Without having to rebuild everything.

Maintenance for a fixed monthly fee

A website is not finished at hand-over. Updates appear, backups need to work and security needs follow-up. If you would rather not deal with that yourself, you can take a maintenance plan for a fixed monthly fee, cancellable monthly with one month's notice. You choose from three options:

Care Basic, €45 per month: hosting, backups, security updates and monitoring, so your site stays fast, safe and online.

Care Plus, €110 per month: everything in Basic, plus 1 hour of small changes per month and a monthly report.

Care Premium, €250 per month: everything in Plus, plus 3 hours of changes, an SEO report and a reply within 1 working day.

If you prefer to pay yearly in advance, you pay 10 months and get 2 free.

All amounts exclude VAT.

? FAQ
How much does a website cost? +

A website starts at €1,250 excl. VAT for a custom site of up to 5 pages. More pages, multiple languages or a webshop need a tailored quote. You always get a fixed price up front.

How much does website maintenance cost? +

Maintenance is optional and costs €45, €110 or €250 per month (excl. VAT), depending on how much follow-up you want. It is cancellable monthly with one month's notice. If you pay yearly in advance, you pay 10 months and get 2 free.

Is maintenance compulsory? +

No. The site is yours and you can also host and maintain it yourself. A maintenance plan is for those who would rather not deal with updates, backups and security.

How long until my website is online? +

For a regular SME site, count on two to four weeks, depending on how fast texts and photos are ready. The exact schedule is in the quote.

Can I still change things myself? +

I make small changes quickly for you. If you want to edit texts regularly yourself, we agree on a solution up front that makes that easy.

Do you only work in the Kempen region? +

No. I live in Meerhout, but I work online for clients across Belgium and beyond. Everything happens remotely: intro call by video, hand-over and explanation, so you have everything digitally right away. If a visit is really needed, I can come by in the Kempen.
