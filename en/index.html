Frequently asked questions

09 FAQ
What does it cost? +

You pay a fixed price per project, agreed in advance. A website starts from €1,250, a chatbot is €129 per month (+ €250 setup) and an automation starts from €750 (excluding VAT). After the free introductory call you get a tailored quote.

I'm not sure what I need. Can I still get in touch? +

Please do. Just describe what bothers you or what you want to achieve. I'll tell you honestly whether a website, a chatbot or an automation fits best, or whether it isn't worth it.

How long does it take to build a chatbot? +

That depends on what it needs to do and how scattered your information is. You do get a first working version to test quickly, though. The exact timing is in the quote, before we start.

Who owns the chatbot after delivery? +

You do. The workflow, the texts and the settings are handed over to you, with an explanation. You're not tied to me.

Where does it run, and what happens to my data? +

That's your choice. In the cloud for speed and convenience, or fully local on a device at your premises, so not a single question leaves your building. We discuss this during the introductory call.

What if the bot gives a wrong answer? +

The bot only answers from what you provide, says so when it doesn't know something, and then refers people to you. Before it goes live, we test it together with the toughest questions. After that, maintenance can keep fine-tuning it.
