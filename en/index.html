01 ABOUT ME

Curious enough to figure it out. Practical enough to build it.

I love diving into technology that isn't obvious yet. I research how things work, combine existing systems and translate technical possibilities into something that feels logical to the person using it.

My work sits at the crossroads of software, artificial intelligence, hardware and creativity. I learn by making, measuring and trying again — until it's right. No slides, just things that work.

Not a classic profile — but one clear common thread: I combine operations, software, AI and hardware into one working system. From professional kitchens and automotive to production, operations and IT, most recently as team lead Production & Operations — it always came down to the same thing: understanding processes, analysing problems and building systems that keep working.

How I work

04 HOW I WORK

Small steps, quickly visible results. That way you know early whether it's right — and surprises stay small.

  1. 01

    Understand

    What really needs solving? I look past the question to the problem behind it.

  2. 02

    Build

    A small, working version — no endless plans, just something you can touch today.

  3. 03

    Test

    With real feedback, real data and real hardware where needed. Measuring instead of guessing.

  4. 04

    Improve

    Refine until it's right, only then expand. That's how you grow something that keeps working.
