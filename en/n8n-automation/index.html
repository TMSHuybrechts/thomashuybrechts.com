What can you automate?

Almost anything that follows a fixed rule. A new enquiry through your website lands automatically in your CRM or Google Sheet and you get a notification on your phone. A quote that is still unsigned after a week gets a friendly reminder by itself. Attachments from your mailbox end up in the right folder, and a new order in your webshop immediately prepares a shipping label.

With AI added it goes a step further: automatically sorting and summarising incoming emails, suggesting a first reply, or extracting data from a PDF invoice.

Why n8n?

n8n is an open-source automation platform that talks to hundreds of apps: Gmail, Outlook, Google Sheets, Excel, Teamleader, Odoo, Shopify, WooCommerce, Slack and almost anything with an API. Unlike Zapier or Make you do not pay per step, and it can run on your own server. That keeps costs low and your data under control.

I use n8n myself every day, among other things for the AI chatbot on this website, which answers questions and sends enquiries to my inbox.
