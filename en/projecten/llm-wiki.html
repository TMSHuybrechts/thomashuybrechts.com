01 THE PROBLEM

Knowledge gets scattered across notes, documents and conversations. I wanted a system that ingests, summarises and connects sources automatically — so the overview grows along instead of fading away.

02 WHAT IT DOES
Automatic ingest
Drop a source in the inbox and the wiki ingests it: summary, key claims, entities and concepts are extracted automatically.
Knowledge graph
Everything is connected in a searchable graph with hundreds of nodes and relations, with separate types for sources, entities, concepts and syntheses.
Living synthesis
An overview page is rewritten on every ingest, so the common thread across all sources stays up to date.
Local & private
Runs on local AI; the entire knowledge base stays on your own machine.
Kept healthy
Built-in checks for orphan pages, broken links and contradictions keep the knowledge base consistent.
03 WHAT I LEARNED
The value wasn't in even more notes, but in the connections between them. Once ingest, graph and synthesis worked together, a loose collection of documents became a real knowledge system.
