01 THE PROBLEM

Good AI images and video aren't a matter of one button. It's in the prompting, choosing the right models, training your own LoRAs and finishing properly. I wanted to master that whole pipeline locally — without a cloud subscription, with full control over style, quality and privacy.

02 WHAT IT DOES
Photorealistic images
From portraits and sculptures to cinematic scenes, with attention to light, composition and detail.
Custom LoRA training
Fine-tuning models to a specific style or character, trained on my own RTX 4070.
ComfyUI workflows
Node-based pipelines for repeatable, controllable results instead of random output.
Video too
The same workflow extended to short moving images — from still to clip.
Fully local
Everything runs on my own machine: no cloud, no subscription, full control and privacy.
03 WHAT I LEARNED
The difference between ‘a picture’ and a usable result is in the workflow: prompting, iterating, training and finishing. Working locally forced me to understand the whole pipeline instead of leaning on an external service.
