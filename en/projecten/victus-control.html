A local-first operations platform to understand, structure and manage an AI workstation: live runtime discovery, inventory, resource monitoring, security review and an intent map that compares your desired setup with reality.

01 THE PROBLEM

A local AI workstation quickly turns into a tangle: models, automations, dashboards and tools spread across processes and ports. What's really running, what should be running, and where does reality drift from your intent?

02 WHAT IT DOES
Live runtime discovery
Scans running services and tools and shows status, health and resource cost per local AI tool — instead of separate terminals and guesswork.
Intent Map — intended vs. observed
Draw how your workstation is supposed to be and let Victus compare that with what's running live: OK, missing, exposed or unexpected. That difference is the core of the project.
Resource monitor
CPU, RAM, GPU and VRAM at a glance, so you immediately see where the pressure is during heavy AI work.
Persistent inventory
Remembers known tools with notes and status labels, even when they're not running — a living catalogue instead of a graveyard.
Security review
Unknown listeners end up in a review flow instead of quietly staying open; you decide what's trusted.
Agents & MCP diagnostics
Visibility into connected agents and MCP servers, on top of a testable React/TypeScript architecture with SQLite state.
03 WHAT I LEARNED
The interesting question wasn't ‘yet another dashboard’, but: let the user define the intended setup and compare it with the live reality. That difference — desired vs. observed — is where the real insight lies.
