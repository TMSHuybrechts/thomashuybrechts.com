Jarvis

A hands-free AI command centre: a visual agent architecture that feels like a tactical game, controlled with claps, voice and hand gestures.

Type
AI command centre
Status
In active development
Role
Concept, design & development
Stack
Local AI · agents · speech (STT) · gesture recognition
RESULT

3 hands-free control modes — clap, voice and hand gestures — on a local brain.

Talk to me about this ↗ Read how it works ↓
01 THE PROBLEM

A chat window is passive. I wanted an assistant you can actually see working — which agents are active, how they cooperate and where the next action comes from — and that you control hands-free.

02 WHAT IT DOES
Agent command centre
A Nexus Core orchestrates specialised agents (research, briefing, memory, risk) — each with its own lane, load and clear input/output. More mission control than admin panel.
Clap control
2× short claps open your dashboards, music and editor; 3× claps ask your knowledge base a spoken question. Fully hands-free.
Voice in Flemish
Voice input and spoken answers in the Dutch spoken here.
Hand gestures
A separate version controls the interface with hand gestures via the camera.
Local brain
Runs on local AI, so your conversations and data stay on your own machine.
03 WHAT I LEARNED
Hands-free control forces you to keep intent simple: a clap, a gesture, a sentence. The real challenge wasn't the features but recognising reliably and giving clear feedback straight away.
