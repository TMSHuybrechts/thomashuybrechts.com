01 THE PROBLEM

AI assistants usually live in a chat window and stop at talking. Sirena is an experiment in the other direction: an assistant you see and hear, in the Dutch spoken here, that can actually do things on your computer.

02 WHAT IT DOES
Visible and audible
An avatar on your desktop, with a Flemish voice. You talk to her, she talks back.
Local brain
The language model runs on your own machine. Your conversations and files never leave your computer.
Real actions
Integrations to open files, launch programs, look up information and carry out tasks — with confirmation where it belongs.
Extensible
Add new skills as modules, so Sirena grows with what you need.
03 WHAT I LEARNED
Voice and avatar instantly make an assistant feel ‘more real’ — and so it gets judged more strictly. Latency, reliability and clear confirmations matter more than yet another feature.
